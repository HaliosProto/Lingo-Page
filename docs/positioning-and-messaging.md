# Positioning and messaging

## Core promise

Understand, communicate, and localize anything in any language—with control over meaning, tone, terminology, privacy, and quality.

## Messaging hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** read and understand multilingual content without losing the page you are using.
2. **Control:** see what is happening, keep originals, recover from partial work, and choose privacy settings.
3. **Quality program:** context, terminology, BiDi correctness, and measured evaluation—not an unsupported “best model” claim.
4. **Platform direction:** move safely from reading to composing, vision, speech, and professional localization as those products become verified.

## Voice

Calm, direct, precise, warm, and internationally understandable. Prefer “Try again in 8 seconds” to raw status codes, “translation service” to provider jargon in default UI, and honest “not yet verified” language to aspirational claims presented as fact.

## Naming system

Use Lingo as the family and outcome-led product names: Page, Compose, Vision, Meet, Studio, Intelligence, Analytics, Platform. Final public name/trademark/domain decisions remain product-owner and legal actions.
