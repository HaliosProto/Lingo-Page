# Translation research experiment template

## Decision question

State the product decision this experiment can change, the user population/languages, and the smallest useful outcome.

## Evidence and rights

- Source/paper, authors, date, identifier, and access date
- Claimed result and known limitations
- Code/data availability and licenses
- Dataset provenance, consent, sensitive content, and allowed uses
- Reproducibility and compute/environment requirements

## Experiment

- Hypothesis and rejection condition
- Synthetic/licensed evaluation corpus and language/domain slices
- Current-product baseline
- Candidate change isolated behind a flag or offline harness
- Automatic metrics with calibration limits
- Human review rubric (meaning, omissions/additions, terminology, tone, entities, placeholders, fluency, BiDi)
- Privacy, security, cost, latency, accessibility, and failure-mode checks
- Sample size/repetitions and analysis plan

## Result and disposition

Record raw artifact locations, summarized results, unexpected failures, reproducibility, and one status: monitor, experiment, adopt, modify, or reject. An adoption still requires an approved product specification, implementation task, verification gate, and rollback.
