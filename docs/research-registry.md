# Translation research registry

This offline seed registry establishes a review process; it is not a literature review or production endorsement. Bibliographic metadata and links must be refreshed from the original paper, standards body, or official project before an experiment because external access was intentionally excluded from Milestone 0.

## Registry fields for new entries

Title, authors, original source, date, stable identifier/link, research area, summary, product relevance, supported languages, claimed results, limitations, reproducibility, code/data availability, licenses, privacy/compute implications, proposed experiment, baseline, status, reviewer, and last-verified date.

## Priority backlog

Omission/hallucination detection, named-entity and placeholder preservation, low-resource languages/dialects/slang, cultural adaptation/transcreation, post-edit learning, simultaneous speech/diarization/code switching, camera/OCR, UI/game localization, and human-in-the-loop workflows. Add only after selecting primary sources and completing the license/privacy screen.
