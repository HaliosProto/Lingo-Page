# Marketing strategy

## Purpose and position

Lingo exists to help people understand, communicate, and localize across languages with control over meaning, tone, terminology, privacy, and quality. It should not be positioned as “another translator with many models.”

Initial wedge: people who read multilingual web content—especially Persian and Arabic users, students, researchers, support workers, and content creators—who need reliable page behavior and understandable recovery. Professional and enterprise audiences follow when Studio/Platform evidence exists.

## Value by audience

| Audience                      | Immediate value                                                | Future value                                      |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Everyday browser user         | Explicit page/selection translation, restore, privacy controls | Durable bilingual reading and history             |
| Persian/Arabic user           | RTL-aware experience and mixed-script quality program          | Specialized terminology/dialect/context workflows |
| Student/researcher            | Preserve page structure; inspect original/translation          | Comparison, explanation, citation-safe workflows  |
| Support/content worker        | Fast controlled understanding                                  | Compose, tone, glossary, feedback                 |
| Translator/agency             | Future handoff from web context                                | Studio, TM, terminology, QA, review, formats      |
| Software/game/enterprise team | Future localization pipeline                                   | Roles, integrations, analytics, regional controls |

## Ethical launch rules

No fake testimonials, invented accuracy percentages, misleading unlimited/privacy/compliance claims, hidden sponsorship, dark patterns, or screenshots containing private content. Every claim maps to a dated verification result. Provider sponsorship cannot alter translation recipient without explicit user control and disclosure.

## Launch prerequisites

Owner acceptance, branded-Chrome/browser evidence, accessibility/BiDi/performance gates, production identity/quotas, provider terms and smoke qualification, accurate privacy policy/store disclosure, support and incident process, release rollback, approved name/domain/brand, synthetic demo corpus, and permission-safe screenshots. Deployment and publication require separate authorization.

## Required assets later

Product narrative, feature/limitation pages, privacy explainer, onboarding, store copy/icons/screenshots, short synthetic demo, comparison methodology, support/troubleshooting, status/incident messaging, and localized Persian/Arabic/English launch material reviewed by native speakers.
